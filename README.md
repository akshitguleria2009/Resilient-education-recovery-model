# Resilient-education-recovery-model
A python project based on disaster hit cities recovering from the possible damages caused to educational institutions specifically and how they can be tackled. Accounting for various factors such as country' economic status, funds alloted previously cost of re construction other reliefs etc. For Asian focus working on the Myanmar Disaster of 2025.

### Model 1
A simple python based program that answers the question of how limited recovery resources can be alloted to to disaster hit areas in the form of both temporary and permanent schools or educational centres.

**28 March 2025, Myanmar Earthquake acts as the center piece on which the model works**

## Research Question 
How does the allocation of limited recovery funding affect the speed of reconstruction for educational institutions in various countries especially developing countries in Asia?


## Background

The 28 March 2025 earthquake caused extensive damage across Myanmar.

The World Bank's GRADE assessment estimated approximately **US$10.97 billion in direct physical damage**.

UNICEF reported that **1,656 schools were assessed across 43 affected townships**, with approximately half damaged and **279 destroyed or rendered non-functional** in that assessment.

Different reports provide different education-damage figures because assessments were conducted at different times and used different definitions and geographic coverage.
